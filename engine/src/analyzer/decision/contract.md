# Decision contract — conclusive outcomes

Pure deterministic computation over the completed AnalysisContext. No runtime
I/O, Detector, Operator, Resolve, or callback to Context. Compare the current
local and remote facts against the confirmed baseline; preserve observation,
history and newly collected facts separately. Exact identity and occupancy are
separate. Name alone, equal size or same timestamp do not prove identity or
content equivalence. A new subject may select creation after Context proves
the exact destination free. Unavailable evidence yields a terminal
InsufficientEvidence diagnosis or Blocked, never a request to another stage.
Conflict blocks all intents for its subject. Compatible independent changes
produce ordered intents. No automatic conflict winner, permanent deletion,
or unauthorized Trash. All target identities and context are preserved.

The models describe requirements even if current collectors cannot provide
them. Context augmentation tests still need realistic data and provenance.
